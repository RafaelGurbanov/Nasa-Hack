**AI-made visuals:** all pictures in the game (Rovi the robot, the 4 astronauts, the Mars scene, the Sun, the storm
effects, the certificate) were drawn in code as SVG/CSS by Claude (`src/art.js`, `src/story.css`). The game's teacher
panel says so. No photos (NASA or other), no image files and no NASA logos are used. Sound effects are generated by
code (`src/sound.js`); there are no audio files. No AI image or video generator was used.
