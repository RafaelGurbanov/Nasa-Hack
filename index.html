Your mission: keep 4 astronauts alive on Mars for 10 sols

You command a small Mars base. A real solar storm is coming. The 10 sols you play replay the real dates , using solar flare and proton records from NASA's DONKI space-weather database.

Each sol you split power units between three systems:

  • Life Support: makes oxygen and recycles water.
  • Greenhouse: grows food.
  • Radiation Shield: protects the crew from storm protons.

Your 4 systems are Life Support (oxygen/water), Energy, Food and Radiation Protection. Energy is shared, and it never covers everything. If oxygen, water or food runs out, or the crew's radiation dose goes over the limit, the mission fails. If that happens, you will see the exact chain of decisions that caused it.

For students aged 12 to 16, in class, in a 10 to 15 minute session. First, a 3-question check (about 1 minute).
